Our Mission

Built for high schoolers ready to build.

High School WHO WE SERVE
Always Free TO PARTICIPATE
“

AIXelerate Challenge is dedicated to breaking down barriers to advanced computer science and technology education. We equip high school students from varied backgrounds with resources, mentorship, and opportunities to build projects that address meaningful problems.

We believe the strongest learning happens when students work on real constraints: users, deadlines, ethics, communication, and teamwork. Our programs are free so that cost does not determine who gets to participate.
